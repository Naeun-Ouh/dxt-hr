## Why leave is last

DXT already has a working leave-management program, so replacing leave is lower urgency than consolidating projects, expenses, assets, onboarding/offboarding, and company operations.

## Delivery strategy

- Finish one vertical slice completely before moving to the next.
- Prefer usable business workflows over broad partial coverage.
- Keep Phase 4 interfaces/routes available, but do not spend implementation effort there until higher-priority phases are complete.
- Do not remove or rewrite approved leave specifications; defer implementation only.
- Every completed phase must pass CI and be reviewed against Figma and business rules before merge.
